How it works

1

Sniff Test

Cheap $0.02 gate via Cloudflare Clef-flash. Is this claim coherent and investigable?

2

Evidence Gathering

Fixtures + live adapters: GPTZero screens for AI text, Alexandria finds primary sources, Sightengine checks images.

3

Photo Forensics

C2PA provenance check → Sightengine AI-generation detection. Fabrication proof becomes evidence.

4

Contrarian Review

An adversarial agent tries to break the verdict before it's finalized.

5

Director Verdict

Weighted evidence → verdict on the Truth-O-Meter scale with confidence score.

6

Human Gate ⛔

Publish, post, and notify are BLOCKED until a human explicitly approves via --approve.
